What Makes OmniDial Different

No extra app switching — seamless phone and OTT messaging together.

💬

Integrated WhatsApp Actions

Initiate standard phone calls or direct WhatsApp voice and message chats directly from any contact row or recent log entry.

⚡

Caller Automation Rules

Set custom automation triggers per caller or group — automatic actions, priority alerts, and flexible call management.

🔍

Predictive T9 Search

Instant predictive search across your contacts and recent call history right from the dial pad as you type.

⭐

Speed Dial & Favorites

Customizable visual favorites grid and rapid long-press key shortcuts (2–9) for instant calling.

📱

Native Default Dialer

Handles incoming, outgoing, and ongoing calls with in-call controls and modern Material 3 Jetpack Compose UI.

🔒

Privacy-First & Ad-Free

Zero telemetry tracking and no advertising. Your contacts and logs remain private on your device.

OmniDial v1.1.1 Latest Release
Build 3 • Released September 2026
Download APK (v1.1.1)
  • T9 Search for Nicknames: Dial pad T9 search now indexes and matches nicknames so you can dial names, nicknames, or numbers interchangeably.
  • Fav & T9 Keypad Layout by Default: Speed Dial Keypad display setting defaults to "Fav & T9", displaying speed dial contact names on top and standard T9 letters below.
  • Stable, Non-Jumping Quick Actions: SMS, WhatsApp Chat, and Secondary Call action buttons remain fixed in place below the keypad with no vertical layout shifts.
  • Automatic Country Code Resolution for WhatsApp/SMS: Automatically queries full stored contact records or appends country calling codes (+91, +1, etc.) to prevent WhatsApp "contact not available" errors.
  • Clean Key 1 Labeling: Eliminated redundant duplicate VM/Voicemail text on Key 1.

  • Cleaner, 4-Button Keypad: Reach anyone faster with 4 organized buttons on your dial pad: Send Text, Regular Phone Call, WhatsApp Message, or WhatsApp Voice Call.
