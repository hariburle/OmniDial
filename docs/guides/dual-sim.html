Master Dual SIM Calling

Two SIMs, zero guesswork. Pick a global SIM mode once, pin specific numbers to a SIM, and let the roaming guard watch your back abroad.

Steps

  1. Choose a SIM mode
  2. Pin a SIM to a specific number
  3. Understand the roaming guard
1

Choose a SIM mode

Pick the mode that matches how you think.

Open Settings and choose one of three modes:

  • System — OmniDial just follows your phone's default SIM. Least clutter, least control.
  • Ask & Learn — OmniDial asks which SIM to use and remembers per contact. Best if your two SIMs serve different parts of your life (work vs. personal).
  • International — domestic calls go out on your primary SIM; only foreign numbers trigger a SIM choice. Best for travelers.

Each number gets its own SIM pill.

Open any contact's details. Under each phone number you'll see SIM pills — tap one to pin that number to SIM 1, SIM 2, Auto, or Always Ask. From then on, calls to that number route through your chosen SIM automatically.

Try this: pin your office contacts' work numbers to your work SIM and family to your personal SIM. You'll never think about SIMs again.
3

Understand the roaming guard

When you travel, OmniDial watches for roaming in real time:

  • If you're about to call on a SIM that's currently roaming, you get an amber warning before the call goes out.
  • Where possible, it steers the call to your non-roaming SIM instead.
  • Incoming calls show which SIM is ringing, with an amber Roaming badge if that SIM is roaming — so you can decide whether to pick up.

Every call in Recents carries a SIM 1 / SIM 2 badge, so your history always shows which line was used.
