Filter between Smart Routing and Call Automation.

Instead of manually picking apps or SIMs every time you place a call, OmniDial evaluates your real-time network environment whenever you tap Call:

  • Where you are: Are you at home, traveling in a specific country, or roaming on a foreign cellular network?
  • Who you are calling: Is it a domestic number, an international number, or a specific country code (+1, +91, +44)?
  • The best route: Automatically launches the call over WhatsApp, Google Voice, or your chosen SIM without extra taps.
2

Global channel preference modes

Under Settings → Channel Preferences, you can set the master policy for how OmniDial treats calling channels:

  • Smart Routing (Recommended): Rules auto-fire. If no rule matches a contact, OmniDial asks once and remembers your preference for that contact.
  • Always Ask: Shows the channel picker dialog on every call. Smart routing rules still evaluate in the background to highlight a ⚡ Suggested by rule recommendation.
  • Cellular Only: Standard carrier calls only. All VoIP rules are bypassed for traditional cellular dialing.
Clean Settings: The Reset Choices and Learn Memory button is automatically hidden when in Cellular Only or Always Ask mode so your settings stay uncluttered.

Create a routing rule in 30 seconds

New Routing Rule
Active Expression Preview:
When @location: Roaming Abroad, route @numbers: +1 via @channel: WhatsApp
@location: Roaming @numbers: +13
@channel: WhatsApp @guard: Auto
Tap any parameter chip to edit its criteria.

1. Open the Rules tab and tap the Smart Routing filter chip.

2. Tap the floating New Routing Rule button. Notice the button is context-sensitive and immediately opens the routing rule builder.

3. Tap any of the four parameter chips to adjust your criteria, then tap Save Rule.

4

Understanding the 4 rule parameters

Every routing rule is configured using four modular parameter slots:

  • @location:
    • Any Location — matches everywhere in the world.
    • Roaming Abroad — matches whenever your SIM connects to a foreign carrier.
    • Traveling in Country — matches when you are physically in a specific ISO country (e.g. IN for India, GB for UK).
    • Home Country — matches only when connected to your home carrier network.
  • @numbers:
    • International — all numbers with a foreign country code.
    • Domestic — local numbers within the country you are calling from.
    • Specific Prefix — matches target dial codes like +1, +91, +44.
  • @channel: Choose which app or line places the call: WhatsApp Personal, WhatsApp Business, SIM 1, SIM 2, or Google Voice.
  • @guard: Choose Auto-Dispatch Silently for instant background routing, or Warn Before Calling to show a quick roaming safety prompt.
5

One-tap recipe templates

Don't want to build a rule from scratch? Tap Recipes at the top of the Rules screen to load pre-tested community templates:

  • Avoid Roaming Back Home: When traveling abroad, route all calls to your home country (+1) over WhatsApp data.
  • Dual-SIM Travel Setup: When in a specific destination country, route all local calls to your local travel SIM.
  • International Friends & Family: Always route foreign calls through WhatsApp to avoid accidental long-distance carrier rates.
Backed Up Safely: All your Smart Routing rules are automatically preserved when you tap Backup Now in Settings, complete with live checksum verification.
