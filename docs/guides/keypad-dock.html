Call the Right Way, Every Time

SIM 1, SIM 2, or WhatsApp — pick your calling channel right from the keypad, then teach OmniDial to remember the choice per contact.

Steps

  1. Meet the channel dock
  2. Switch channels before you dial
  3. Teach it with "Remember choice"
  4. Change a remembered choice
1

Meet the channel dock

The channel dock sits above the dial pad.

Open the Keypad tab and look just above the number keys: that's the channel dock. It shows every way you can place a call — your SIM cards by carrier name, and WhatsApp — with badges like Roaming when relevant.

2

Switch channels before you dial

Tap a channel in the dock, then dial or tap a suggestion as usual — the call goes out on the channel you picked. That's it for one-off calls: traveling and want this one call on WhatsApp instead of your roaming SIM? One tap, done.

3

Teach it with "Remember choice"

Pick a channel and tick Remember choice.

When you call a contact that has more than one way to reach them, OmniDial asks with a Choose Calling Method dialog. Pick the channel — and if this is how you always call this person, turn on Remember choice. From then on, tapping their name dials that way instantly, no dialog.

Try this: set your family to WhatsApp and your office contacts to cellular. After a day of teaching, the dialer just does the right thing.
4

Change a remembered choice

Changed your mind? Open the contact's details and update the preferred channel / SIM on their number — the next call follows the new choice. For the full picture on per-number SIM pinning and SIM modes, see the dual-SIM guide.

Car & hands-free: when you call from Android Auto or a Bluetooth headset, OmniDial automatically uses the contact's remembered channel — no fumbling with your phone while driving.
