Frequently Asked Questions

The things people ask before — and after — installing OmniDial.

Does OmniDial replace my phone's dialer?
Yes — that's the point. When you set it as your default phone app, it handles incoming and outgoing calls, your call history, and contacts, just like the dialer that came with your phone. You can switch back to your old dialer in Android Settings at any time.
Is my data private? Where does it go?
Everything stays on your phone. Contacts, call logs, rules, favorites, and backups live in local storage — there is no account, no cloud sync, and no ads. Backups are files you control in your Documents/OmniDial/ folder.
Why does it ask for SMS permission?
Only for automation rules you create yourself — for example, a gate-buzzer rule that texts you a confirmation after opening the door. OmniDial never sends messages on its own.
Do I need WhatsApp installed for WhatsApp calling?
Yes. OmniDial hands WhatsApp calls and chats to the WhatsApp app on your phone. If WhatsApp isn't installed, those options simply won't do anything.
Does it work with two SIM cards?
Yes — dual SIM is a first-class feature. Choose a global SIM mode in Settings (System, Ask & Learn, or International), pin individual numbers to a SIM, and the roaming guard warns you before you call on a roaming line. See the dual-SIM guide.
How do automation rules work? Will it answer calls without me?
Only for numbers you explicitly configure. A rule matches an incoming number you specify (like your building's intercom) and runs the steps you defined — answer, send tones, hang up. Nothing runs for other callers. Every trigger is logged under the History icon in the Rules tab, and you can test any rule with the dry-run simulator first.
Will my contacts and call history carry over?
Yes. On first launch OmniDial imports your existing contacts and call history automatically — nothing to migrate by hand.
What happens to my data if I uninstall?
The app's internal data is removed, but your backups in Documents/OmniDial/ survive — reinstall later and restore in one tap. Your system contacts and system call log are untouched by uninstalling.
Which Android versions are supported?
Android 11 and newer.
It's not working as my default dialer / calls aren't connecting. What do I check?
First, confirm OmniDial is set as the default phone app (Android Settings → Apps → Default apps → Phone app). Then check it has Phone, Contacts, and Call log permissions. On some Samsung/Xiaomi devices, also exempt OmniDial from battery optimization so background call handling isn't killed.
