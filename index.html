Unique Capabilities & Architectural Innovations

Built for users who demand power, precision, and streamlined communication from their phone app.

🔀
Telecom & OTT

Dual-Channel Calling & Car Redirection

Unify Cellular GSM and WhatsApp calling into one frictionless interface. In "Ask & Learn" mode, OmniDial learns how you communicate with each contact and highlights your preferred route.

  • Automated Car Redirection: Calls initiated from Android Auto or Bluetooth headsets auto-route via WhatsApp when preferred.
  • Smart Country Prefixing: Automatically prepends country codes so overseas messaging never fails.
🎧
In-Call Audio

Pre-Answer Audio Route Selector

Take complete command of where sound goes before accepting an incoming call. Never accidentally answer on speaker in public or scramble to pair your car headset mid-conversation.

  • Pre-Answer HUD: Select Handset Earpiece, Speakerphone, or paired Bluetooth devices right on the incoming ring screen.
  • Bluetooth Device Recognition: Instant routing to AirPods, Galaxy Buds, hearing aids, or vehicle dashboards.
🤖
Smart Automation

Automated Gate & Intercom Buzzer Rules

A dedicated automation engine tailored for apartment buzzers, security gates, and delivery intercoms. Never get locked out or miss delivery packages again.

  • Auto-Answer Delay: Answers gate calls automatically after a configured delay (e.g. 1 second).
  • Automated DTMF Key Sequence: Transmits touch tones (e.g. 9# or *#) to buzz open the door.
  • Carrier Spam Bypass: Whitelists intercom numbers so STIR/SHAKEN filters never drop deliveries.
🎹
Dialer Keypad

Quick Actions for Unsaved Numbers

Dial any number—temporary contractors, local businesses, or delivery couriers not in your phonebook—and immediately access a structured 4-action hub.

  • 4 Instant Actions: Regular Phone Call, Send Text (SMS), WhatsApp Chat, or WhatsApp Voice Call.
  • No Address Book Clutter: Message or VoIP-call anyone on WhatsApp without saving them to contacts first.
📶
Dual-SIM & Travel

Dual-SIM Intelligence & Roaming Guard

Master multi-SIM devices with granular per-number routing and real-time international travel protection against unexpected roaming fees.

  • Per-Number Preferred SIM: Assign SIM 1, SIM 2, Auto, or Always Ask to specific numbers within multi-number contacts.
  • Roaming-Aware Protection: Detects network roaming and automatically routes calls to local eSIMs or VoIP channels.
  • Call Log SIM Badges: Visual SIM 1 and SIM 2 badges displayed directly on past call entries.
📝
Productivity

Post-Call Notes & Scheduled Reminders

Capture key discussion points and action items right when a call finishes, with seamless reminders that keep your follow-ups on track.

  • Instant Post-Call Sheet: Quick note entry appears after hanging up to record meeting items while fresh.
  • Scheduled Notification Alarms: Set 15-minute, 1-hour, or next-morning reminders with one-tap callback buttons.
  • Persistent Log Attachments: Notes remain permanently attached to call history records.
👂
In-Call Sensors

Smart Screen-Off Ear Detection

Intelligent route-aware proximity screen control that turns off the display when held to your ear, preventing accidental cheek presses or muted calls.

  • Route-Aware Proximity Off: Disables screen when held to ear; keeps screen awake when on speakerphone or Bluetooth.
  • Automatic Sensor Re-engagement: Re-engages automatically if audio switches from hands-free back to the handset earpiece.
🛡️
Spam Defense

STIR/SHAKEN Spam Defense & Screening

Detect carrier spoofing, warn against fraudulent callers, and automatically screen telemarketers before your phone rings.

  • STIR/SHAKEN Verification: Warns when caller ID authentication fails due to carrier spoofing.
  • Tiered Caller Trust Badges: Visual trust ratings for Verified Business, Delivery Couriers, and High-Risk Spam.
  • Auto-Block Presets: Pre-emptively drops persistent robocallers and suspicious incoming patterns.
💾
Data Backup

Cryptographic One-Tap Verified Backup

Easily protect and migrate your dialer configurations, caller rules, custom favorite grids, and speed dial shortcuts across Android devices.

  • 1-Tap Local Backup: Exports all preferences to clean JSON without navigating confusing folder pickers.
  • SHA-256 Checksums: Cryptographic integrity validation ensures backup files are untampered and uncorrupted.
  • Smooth Device Migration: Rapidly restore your personalized setup when upgrading to a new phone.
⏳
Telephony Tools

Pause (,) & Wait (;) IVR Touch-Tone Dialing

Navigate complex automated phone trees, corporate extension directories, and conference dial-ins effortlessly with standardized pause syntax.

  • Quick Key Shortcuts: Long-press * for a 2-second Pause (,) and # for a Wait (;).
  • Automated Tree Navigation: Dial extensions, PIN codes, and meeting IDs automatically without manual re-entry.
🔍
Contact Discovery

Discovery Filters & Deep Search

Surfacing the right person at the right time with smart discovery categories and lightning-fast predictive indexing.

  • Rediscover Dormant Contacts: Instantly highlights friends and colleagues you haven't contacted in over 60 days.
  • Zero-Lag T9 Search: Matches full names, initials, nicknames, and digits in real time as you type.
