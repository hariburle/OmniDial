Quick Answers

Common questions, short answers. The full list lives on the FAQ page.

Does OmniDial replace my phone's dialer?
Yes — set it as your default phone app and it handles calls, history, and contacts like your stock dialer. Switch back anytime in Android Settings.
Is my data private?
Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud sync, no ads. Backups are files you control.
Do I need WhatsApp installed for WhatsApp calling?
Yes — OmniDial hands WhatsApp calls to the WhatsApp app on your phone.
